Built around your server

Nookast talks to your own PinePods server and keeps everything in step: progress, played state, favourites and the Up next queue, on any number of devices.

🛟

It always plays

Can't reach a podcast's host from your phone? Nookast streams through your server, or has it fetch the episode, and carries on from the same second.

🧭

Never loses your place

Every tap is kept on the phone until the server confirms it. No signal, a reboot, a killed app: nothing is lost, and a stale server answer never overwrites a newer change.

✨

Chapters everywhere

From the feed, from the file with their own artwork, or recognised from plain timestamps in the description.

🪦

Dead shows stand out

Covers say «Quiet for 6 months», «Abandoned» or «Feed unavailable». A feed moved? Change its RSS address in place, progress stays.

🎨

Made to be looked at

Five palettes in light and dark, colours from the cover, skies that follow the real sun where you are, and a New Year mood with snow.

🔒

Private

No analytics, ads or trackers. Releases are built in a public workflow, started on an emulator and checked by SHA-256 and signature.

The pill 📡

The small capsule under the timeline in Now Playing is the source pill. It tells where the sound comes from right now — a file on the phone, the copy on your PinePods server, the podcast's own host or a stream through the server — and how the connection is doing: a green dot means all is well. Tap it to see quality and buffer, check the connection, and play through your server when the host is out of reach.

A closer look

Phones, foldables, tablets and large displays, with two panes where there is room.

Install in a minute

  1. Download nookast-<version>.apk from the latest release and open it.
  2. Sign in with your PinePods server address, login and password.
  3. That's it. Nookast updates itself from the releases, once a day over Wi-Fi while nothing plays, or just tells you, as you choose.

По-русски

Нукаст — плеер подкастов для вашего сервера PinePods. Он помнит, где вы остановились, и на любом устройстве продолжает с того же места. Если подкаст недоступен напрямую, звук пойдёт через ваш сервер. Таймкоды из описания превращаются в главы, заброшенные подкасты видно по пометке на обложке, а всё, что вы делаете, сначала сохраняется на телефоне и не теряется без сети.

Пилюля — небольшая плашка под шкалой времени в плеере. По ней видно, откуда сейчас идёт звук и как дела со связью. Если на неё нажать, откроются подробности: качество, запас буфера, проверка связи и кнопка, чтобы слушать через сервер, когда подкаст с телефона недоступен.
